# Focused primary-literature follow-up — 16 November 2026

This is a targeted check, not exhaustive novelty clearance. It narrows the
working paper's claims following E3 and adds a field-data path.

- [Hendrickx et al., Machine learning with a reject option](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.11277)
  reviews established rejection architectures and evaluation. Abstention and
  learned selection are not new contributions by themselves.
- [The Craft of Selective Prediction, EMNLP Findings 2024](https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-emnlp.208/)
  includes multi-label selective classification. Instance-level ecological
  community decisions need to be distinguished from label-wise rejection; do not
  claim generic novelty for adapting uncertainty to a multi-label task.
- [Attia et al., Uncertainty-Based Referral for Selective Chest X-Ray Classification, 2026](https://papers.miccai.org/miccai-2026-sat/paper/MIRASOL_017.pdf)
  reports useful ensemble-entropy referral on a medical multi-label task. Its
  study-level signal differs from our selected-set entropy. This is a useful
  counterpoint: our operational failure does not show that all entropy measures
  or multi-label referral methods fail.
- [Gneiting, Making and Evaluating Point Forecasts](https://arxiv.org/abs/0912.0902)
  establishes why point-forecast targets and scores must agree. Our quality MSE
  estimates a conditional mean of observed F1; it is not a binary Brier score.
  Training-mean controls and simple MSE identities are established tools, not
  new theory.
- [Gneiting and Resin, Regression Diagnostics meets Forecast Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.03210)
  places regression calibration, reliability diagnostics and skill in a broader
  established framework. Any decomposition-based contribution needs a specific
  empirical SDM result rather than a renamed calibration identity.

Previously identified [SDC](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10994-026-07096-w),
[SAGE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31082) and
[RankNet](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/learning-to-rank-using-gradient-descent/)
remain substantive precedents. SDC's ratio, sampling-aware species evaluation and
pairwise ranking are not our inventions.

The viable contribution candidate is a controlled SDM evaluation that separates
magnitude skill, acceptance ordering, representation and ecological service
across survey domains, with strong baselines and failed confirmations preserved.
E3 prevents a general scalar-calibration superiority claim. A separate NEON
evaluation requires the [documented predictor/taxonomy bridge](neon_confirmation_feasibility.md)
before composition outcomes or any new model result. No target venue or paper
acceptance prediction follows from this review.
