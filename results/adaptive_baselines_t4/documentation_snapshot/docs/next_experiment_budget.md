## Decision after T4

The [T4 matched-baseline audit](adaptive_baselines_t4_results.md) completes the
second package of the earlier four-package roadmap. Its 300 executed CPU
trajectories / 360 priced paths do not establish a new acquisition algorithm.
For equal-quota policies, static paired width is ordinary width on the same
verification scope up to a constant. Updating widths reduces charged costs;
learning the mismatch forecast has little consistent incremental value. The

below the frozen 10% target in all four nonzero-error/price strata.

**Two proposed packages remain:** model/source replication, then separately
uninspected external confirmation with a record/cost audit. They serve a narrow

## Package 1: model and survey-source replication

Question: do the measurement artifacts and verification-cost tradeoffs persist
when fixed predictions, acceptance disagreements and recorded survey inventories
change? Use existing verified outputs wherever possible. At least two additional
prediction settings and another survey source are proposed; these are replication
blocks, not new independent confirmations if their outcomes were already seen.

Before replay, inventory available prediction arrays, target/native universes,
current-record semantics, genus links, model/reference provenance and spatial
support. Check whether the source supplies enough disagreement cells, recorded
positives and independent clusters to answer the question. A source that fails
the observation contract is a feasibility failure, not a ready confirmation set.
The failed E4/A3u cohort cannot be renamed adequate.

Freeze settings, fixed generic controls, margins, error/cost scenarios and a
compute ceiling after semantic tests, before opening any new outcomes. Include
the simpler adaptive-width and fixed-forecast controls; do not select only the
learned-cost arm. Separate effects of fixed predictions from acceptance-policy
changes. Report all primary results, cost/order endpoints, label validity and
representation/inventory tradeoffs. Require independent operation-cost and
stopping-boundary reconstruction as in T4.

**Planning scale, not an admitted budget:** retaining the entire T4 grid for
three distinct prediction/cohort blocks would give 3 × 360 = 1,080 priced paths,
or 900 actually executed trajectories with the same price-independent reuse.
This arithmetic is not 1,080 new scientific experiments and is not a commitment
to that grid. Feasibility may justify a smaller prespecified comparison. Exact
settings/counts and the CPU ceiling remain unfrozen; the next action is the
input/contract inventory. The allocated new backbone-fit budget remains **zero**.
Only reference-validated missing inference may use the existing bounded private
GPU authorization; CPU query simulation does not need a GPU test.

**Decision:** if effects are unstable, publish the variation and narrow the claim.
If no supported second source/settings exist, record the limitation and discuss
an empirical case-study scope with the supervisor rather than silently filling
the table with unsuitable data.

## Package 2: uninspected external outcomes and a record/cost audit

Question: can a frozen, practically meaningful observation/query contract be
used on separately uninspected observations, and what does its verification
actually cost? Admission must precede outcome access. Audit source identity,
training/support overlap, existing label consumption, taxonomic authority,
current-record completeness, outside-target meanings and clustered sample size.
A new split or local timestamp does not establish independence. A new source can
still overlap backbone training support; state exactly what is held out.

A real record/cost component should check whether group membership, native/genus
constraints and binary compatibility audits can be supported from available
records. Report unresolved cases and audit mistakes. Measure identification and
audit effort only if such observation is actually feasible; simulation ratios
are not substitutes for measurements. Do not assume a perfect human audit or
true occupancy from recorded inventories. If a field process cannot be accessed,
label this as missing evidence and narrow the paper's scope.

Freeze one justified claim, controls, practical margin, sample adequacy and
analysis before accessing these outcomes. The external sample size and any
annotation budget are **not yet established**. No field sampling, paid annotation
or new neural-fit budget has been committed. Reuse validated predictions or
perform only necessary reference-checked inference. No external release is
currently admitted as fresh confirmation.

**Decision:** a failed admission or failed result remains publishable evidence
only within its actual role. Do not rescue it through another mask, split name
or post-hoc endpoint. A broader claim requires new adequate evidence.

## Writing and stopping rule

Write alongside replication. Center the paper on how observation contracts and
charged verification alter ecological model comparison; report failed primaries
and conditional effects plainly. Keep broad competition history and less central
reliability prototypes in the thesis or supplement. The standalone static-score
method branch is closed for this setting. A strong new-method paper is not
supported by the current evidence; a carefully positioned empirical paper remains
plausible, subject to novelty review, replication and suitable validation.

Earlier five-study proposals containing 18+6 future neural fits are superseded,
not outstanding obligations. Their historical text remains in frozen
study/publication documentation snapshots. Current work is governed by the two
packages above. Do not expand compute to repair an unfavorable primary result.
