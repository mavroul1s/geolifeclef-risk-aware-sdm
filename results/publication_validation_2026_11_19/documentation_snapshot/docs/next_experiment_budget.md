## Why more work is needed

R1 establishes a useful development signal beyond metadata under equal coverage,
but fails regional non-regression. The advantage over mean confidence is smaller
than over metadata, disagreement adds no clear incremental utility in post-hoc
ablations, and rare-species recovery is not improved over simple confidence.
Existing PA labels have all been used historically. Further neural training on
those labels cannot create independent confirmation.

The next objective is a defensible claim about decision-aligned reliability,
regional service and ecological coverage. A new algorithm is not assumed to be
necessary or successful. A rigorous empirical paper is possible if a specific
finding replicates and contributes beyond existing literature; acceptance and
novelty are not guaranteed by any experiment count.

## Five proposed studies

An **experiment family** is a scientific comparison. A **neural fit** trains one
model for one seed and training split. A notebook may contain many fits. CPU
selector/calibration fits are separate and much cheaper. The completed full R1
notebook was one study containing six neural fits, not six separate discoveries.

| Study | Question and fixed comparisons to design | Reuse / proposed new neural fits |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Confidence and species-set decisions | Compare simple confidence, raw entropy, metadata/distance and lean error prediction across top-k, calibrated-threshold and expected-F1-surrogate decoders. Refit decoder-specific error targets on risk-calibration rows only. Distinguish richness MAE from F1. | Saved predictions; **0** new neural fits |
| 2. Value and cost of ensembles | Compare individual saved members with their mean ensemble; separate averaging's prediction benefit from disagreement's selector benefit. Export per-member confidence and preprocessing for reuse. | Six saved checkpoints; one bounded inference-only job if needed; **0** new neural fits |
| 3. Regional and ecological utility | Compare the lean pooled model with a small prespecified regional/shrinkage or calibration-only fallback family. Audit identical regional/area coverage, rare occurrence coverage and explicit abstention/referral costs. | Existing confidence/label tables and CPU fits; **0** new neural fits |
| 4. Replication across models and spatial domains | Freeze two model families, three geographic assessment domains and three individual-model seeds; compare the selected diagnostic/method against strong controls with the same fits. | **2 × 3 × 3 = 18** new neural fits |
| 5. Independent confirmation | On a genuinely nonoverlapping labeled source, train two suitable model families with three seeds and one prespecified held-out assessment cohort; lock the method and analysis before outcomes are opened. | Initial budget **2 × 3 = 6** new neural fits; feasibility-dependent |

**Working budget: five experiment families, zero new neural training for the
first three, and up to 24 new neural fits if both later stages are admitted.**
This excludes the six completed R1 fits and does not mean 24 notebooks, 24
architecture choices or 24 independent data sets. It is a planning allocation,
not a minimum required for publication. Smaller useful studies may stop earlier.
If data feasibility or the final protocol requires a different design, revise
and document the budget before training rather than silently expanding it.

For study 4, report seed variability of each individual-model pipeline. If members
are also averaged, report ensemble performance separately: three members do not
constitute three independent ensemble replications. Previously used PA domains
remain development replications even under a new geographic partition. Select
domains using covariates and support, not observed favorable outcomes.

Studies 1–3 are partly retrospective by design. Use training/calibration-only
inner geographic splits to choose any new rules; do not tune a Swiss-specific
exception on its already inspected assessment loss. Lock a small comparison set
before further assessment. Preserve R1's failed safeguard rather than changing
the threshold to declare success.

## Admission and stopping decisions

1. Begin with the cheap diagnostic studies and independent-data feasibility audit
   in parallel. No additional architecture sweep is needed. The saved mean
   probabilities are float16; exploratory decoder comparisons must disclose this.
   Exact ranking-sensitive results require regenerated higher-precision inference
   and checks against saved primary scores. Preprocessing objects were not
   exported and must be reconstructed from frozen training data for checkpoint use.
2. Before study 4, state the exact finding to replicate and identify its strongest
   baseline. If a more complex selector adds no practical benefit over lean
   confidence, stop that method branch. A replicated diagnostic finding can still
   motivate an empirical paper, but do not continue tuning merely to manufacture
   a positive new-method result. Set improvement, regional regression and ecological
   coverage margins prospectively; R1 supplies planning evidence, not a new test.
3. Freeze spatial domains, models, compute limits, primary contrasts and analysis
   before the replication fits. Smoke-time each distinct model/data path and admit
   training in bounded batches. R1's 69-minute runtime cannot accurately price a
   different architecture or external data pipeline. Do not promise a GPU-hour
   total before those measurements.
4. Audit independent data early. sPlotOpen publicly provides vegetation composition
   and survey/environmental metadata, making it a candidate rather than a verified
   independent test. Audit sources, duplicate/resampled plots, spatial and temporal
   overlap, survey completeness, licenses and predictor availability. Exclude
   composition-derived traits from predictors. Do not try to map protected
   anonymous GeoPlant taxa to named species. A dataset-specific label space can
   test method transfer if a compatible design is feasible. If it is not, choose
   another source or explicitly narrow the paper's claims; do not relabel old
   GeoLifeCLEF data as independent.
5. Study 5's six-fit budget assumes one audited source and compatible predictors;
   it does not include an unknown data-acquisition project or guarantee adequate
   statistical precision. Have a supervisor hold the final outcomes if possible.
   If effects remain inconsistent, report the limitation or change the paper scope;
   further seed sweeps do not substitute for independent data.

An optional v35 leaderboard attempt is outside this core paper budget. It should
not delay the reliability study and needs a separate measured admission decision.
Beating the old winner remains an optional objective.
